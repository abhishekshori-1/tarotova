**SEO approach.** The chosen names are short, readable, contain the topic word, and avoid hyphens or numbers. These are branding and usability advantages, not a promise of ranking. Google says domain words are one of many relevance signals and its exact-match-domain system limits disproportionate credit for query-matching names. See [Google's ranking-system documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ranking-systems-guide). Build search visibility through useful original public content, accurate titles, accessible pages, and good performance. Keep personal readings private and out of the index.
